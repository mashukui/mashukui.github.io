蒲公英达人筛选思路：如何建立可复核的候选名单

2026-11-22 · 马哥数据采集

从品牌目标、达人画像、内容证据、合作指标到候选表管理，介绍达人筛选的基本方法。

一、从品牌目标反推筛选条件

先确定推广目标是品牌曝光、产品种草、线索转化还是内容共创。目标不同，达人类型、内容形式和评估指标也不同。

二、画像维度要可解释

可考虑内容垂类、受众地域、账号风格、更新稳定性、历史合作内容和预算区间。每个维度都应说明为何与当前项目有关，避免把粉丝数当作唯一门槛。

三、候选名单需要证据

记录账号主页、代表性笔记、公开合作信息、筛选日期和核验状态。对于报价、档期或合作政策等易变信息，应以平台当前展示或双方确认的信息为准，不要沿用旧表格中的历史数字。

四、评估内容匹配度

  • 内容主题是否与产品使用场景一致。
  • 近期内容是否持续、质量是否稳定。
  • 评论区讨论是否与目标受众相关。
  • 历史商业内容是否自然且符合品牌要求。

五、形成可执行的分层

可以分为优先沟通、继续观察、暂不匹配三类，并记录理由和复核日期。最终合作名单应由人工审核，确认报价、排期、权益和合规要求。

达人筛选涉及商业决策，公开互动指标只能作为参考，不能据此断言真实受众构成或保证投放效果。

Xiaohongshu Pugongying Creator Selection: Building a Verifiable Shortlist

2026-11-22 · Mage Data Tools

An overview of creator selection based on brand goals, creator profiles, content evidence, collaboration metrics, and candidate-list management.

1. Work Backward from Brand Goals

First decide whether the campaign aims to increase awareness, seed product interest, generate leads, or co-create content. Different goals require different creator types, content formats, and evaluation metrics.

2. Make Profile Criteria Explainable

Consider content category, audience region, account style, publishing consistency, past sponsored content, and budget range. Explain why each dimension matters to the project rather than using follower count as the only threshold.

3. Support Every Candidate with Evidence

Record the profile, representative posts, public collaboration information, selection date, and verification status. For changing information such as rates, availability, or collaboration policies, rely on the platform's current display or directly confirmed details rather than old figures in a spreadsheet.

4. Evaluate Content Fit

  • Are the content themes aligned with the product's use cases?
  • Are recent posts consistent in frequency and quality?
  • Are comment discussions relevant to the target audience?
  • Does previous sponsored content feel natural and meet brand requirements?

5. Create Actionable Tiers

Group candidates into priority outreach, continue monitoring, and not currently suitable, with reasons and review dates. A human review should confirm rates, schedules, deliverables, and compliance requirements before a final collaboration decision.

Creator selection involves commercial decisions. Public engagement metrics are only references; they cannot prove audience composition or guarantee campaign performance.
