小红书 · 实战教程

小红书笔记与评论数据，应该如何整理？

2026-11-22 · 马哥数据采集

介绍关键词研究、笔记筛选、评论整理和数据复核的基本思路，帮助建立清晰、可追溯的数据表。

一、从业务问题拆解关键词

不要一开始就堆叠大量宽泛词。先列出品牌、产品类别、使用场景、痛点和竞品等词组，再根据实际搜索结果调整。记录每组关键词的目的，避免后续无法解释数据来源。

二、建立笔记筛选规则

建议记录笔记链接、标题或文本摘要、发布时间、互动指标和采集日期。筛选时结合内容相关性与发布时间，不要只按点赞量排序；互动量高不一定意味着内容与研究目标相关。

三、评论数据如何归档

  • 保留评论对应的笔记链接，避免评论脱离上下文。
  • 把评论文本与时间等字段分开存储，方便筛选。
  • 对同一笔记的评论设置稳定的关联标识。
  • 对重复、空白和异常内容设置明确的清洗规则。

四、建议的工作表结构

笔记表：笔记链接、关键词、发布时间、内容主题、互动指标、采集时间。

评论表：笔记链接、评论文本、评论时间、必要的公开互动指标、采集时间。

将笔记表和评论表分开，能避免一篇笔记对应多条评论时重复堆叠笔记信息。

五、质量复核与合规

抽样打开原笔记，检查内容是否仍然可见、字段是否错位、数据是否重复。使用数据时遵守平台规则和适用法律；未经授权不要尝试访问非公开内容，也不要将可识别个人身份的信息用于无关目的。

建议把原始导出文件设为只读备份，在副本中完成清洗和分析，便于复核与追踪。

Xiaohongshu · Practical Guide

How to Organize Xiaohongshu Posts and Comment Data

2026-11-22 · Mage Data Tools

A practical introduction to keyword research, post filtering, comment organization, and data validation for clear and traceable datasets.

1. Turn Business Questions into Keywords

Do not begin by collecting a large number of broad terms. Start with groups such as brands, product categories, use cases, pain points, and competitors, then refine them based on actual search results. Record the purpose of each keyword group so the data source remains explainable.

2. Set Rules for Selecting Posts

Consider recording the post URL, title or text summary, publication date, engagement metrics, and collection date. Evaluate relevance together with recency rather than sorting only by likes. High engagement does not necessarily mean that a post is relevant to your research.

3. Organize Comment Data

  • Keep the URL of the post associated with each comment so it is not separated from its context.
  • Store comment text and timestamps in separate fields for easier filtering.
  • Use a stable key to associate comments with the same post.
  • Define explicit rules for duplicates, blank records, and abnormal content.

4. Recommended Workbook Structure

Posts sheet: post URL, keyword, publication date, topic, engagement metrics, and collection date.

Comments sheet: post URL, comment text, comment timestamp, relevant public engagement metrics, and collection date.

Keeping posts and comments in separate sheets avoids repeating post information when one post has many comments.

5. Quality Review and Compliance

Open a sample of the original posts to check visibility, field alignment, and duplicates. Follow platform rules and applicable laws. Do not attempt to access non-public content without authorization or use identifiable personal information for unrelated purposes.

Keep the original export as a read-only backup, then clean and analyze a copy. This makes review and traceability easier.
