抖音视频数据采集：从视频链接到结构化数据表

2026-11-22 · 马哥数据采集

介绍抖音视频数据整理的目标拆解、视频清单、字段设计、采集质量检查和结果归档方法。

一、先定义采集范围

先确定研究主题、时间范围和视频来源。可以从关键词检索结果、指定账号公开主页或已整理的视频链接开始。建议先做小样本验证，再扩大范围，避免把大量无关内容带入后续分析。

二、建立视频清单

为每条视频保留唯一链接或平台标识，并记录发现来源、关键词、采集日期和筛选理由。链接是回溯原始内容的重要依据，不应只保存标题或截图。

三、设计字段

  • 基础信息：视频链接、视频标题或文本摘要、作者公开标识、发布时间。
  • 内容信息：话题标签、描述文本、视频时长等实际可用字段。
  • 互动信息：点赞、评论、分享等公开指标，并注明采集时间。
  • 任务信息：搜索词、来源批次、采集状态和异常备注。

四、做好数据质量检查

检查链接是否重复、字段是否缺失、数字是否被错误识别为文本，以及互动指标是否来自同一时间快照。抽样打开原视频核对标题、作者和发布时间。动态指标会变化，不能把不同日期的数据直接当作同一时点比较。

只处理有权访问的公开内容，遵守平台规则和适用法律。不要尝试绕过登录、权限或访问控制。

五、归档与分析

保留原始导出文件，再在副本中清洗。将视频表与评论表通过视频标识关联，可以进一步分析内容主题与用户反馈之间的关系。

Douyin · Video Data

Douyin Video Data Collection: From URLs to Structured Data

2026-11-22 · Mage Data Tools

Learn how to define collection goals, build a video list, design fields, check data quality, and archive results.

1. Define the Collection Scope

Set the research topic, time range, and video sources first. You may begin with keyword search results, public creator profiles, or a prepared list of video URLs. Validate a small sample before expanding to avoid bringing irrelevant content into later analysis.

2. Build a Video Inventory

Keep a unique URL or platform identifier for each video, along with its discovery source, keyword, collection date, and selection rationale. URLs are essential for tracing original content; titles or screenshots alone are not enough.

3. Design the Fields

  • Basic information: video URL, title or text summary, public creator identifier, and publication date.
  • Content information: hashtags, description, duration, and other fields actually available.
  • Engagement information: public likes, comments, and shares, with collection timestamps.
  • Task information: search terms, source batch, collection status, and exception notes.

4. Check Data Quality

Check duplicate URLs, missing fields, numbers incorrectly parsed as text, and whether engagement metrics belong to the same snapshot. Open a sample of original videos to verify titles, creators, and publication dates. Dynamic metrics change over time, so data from different dates should not be compared as if captured simultaneously.

Process only public content you are authorized to access, and follow platform rules and applicable laws. Do not bypass login, permissions, or access controls.

5. Archive and Analyze

Keep the raw export and perform cleaning on a copy. Linking video and comment tables through a video identifier makes it possible to analyze relationships between content topics and audience feedback.
