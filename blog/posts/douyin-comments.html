如何整理抖音视频评论并导出 Excel？

2026-11-22 · 马哥数据采集

从确定研究目标、准备视频链接，到采集结果检查、字段整理与表格分析，梳理一套可复用的抖音评论数据工作流程。

一、先明确研究目标

开始整理评论前，先明确用途：了解用户对某个产品的反馈、归纳视频讨论主题，还是追踪公开话题的讨论变化。目标不同，需要关注的字段和筛选方式也不同。

二、准备视频范围与采集条件

  1. 优先整理与研究主题直接相关的视频链接，并记录来源与采集日期。
  2. 检查视频是否仍然公开可访问，以及评论是否对当前账号可见。
  3. 控制采集范围和频率，遵守平台规则、适用法律及个人信息保护要求。

三、检查导出数据

将数据导出为 CSV 或 Excel 后，先检查字段完整性、重复记录，以及时间、文本和互动字段是否符合预期。不要仅凭采集条数判断数据质量。

建议关注的字段

  • 视频链接或视频标识：追溯评论来源。
  • 评论文本：用于主题归纳和人工复核。
  • 评论时间：观察讨论变化。
  • 公开互动数据：辅助判断讨论热度，注意指标定义。
  • 采集时间：记录数据快照时间，便于比较。

四、在 Excel 中做基础整理

  1. 删除完全重复的记录，但保留原始数据备份。
  2. 按视频、时间或关键词筛选评论。
  3. 将明显无关、空白或异常内容标记出来，并保留清洗规则。
  4. 抽样回看原视频，核对数据与上下文。
评论内容可能包含个人信息。分析和分享时应遵循最小必要原则，避免公开传播可识别个人身份的信息。

五、形成可复用的流程

每次任务都保留关键词、视频清单、采集日期、导出文件和清洗规则。后续更新数据时，才能比较不同时间段的结果。

How to Organize Douyin Video Comments and Export Them to Excel

2026-11-22 · Mage Data Tools

A reusable workflow for defining research goals, preparing video URLs, checking collected data, organizing fields, and analyzing results in spreadsheets.

1. Define Your Research Goal

Before organizing comments, clarify what you want to learn: customer feedback about a product, discussion themes around a video, or changes in public conversations over time. Different goals require different fields and filtering rules.

2. Prepare Video Scope and Collection Conditions

  1. List videos directly related to your topic and record their sources and collection dates.
  2. Check whether each video is still publicly accessible and whether its comments are visible to your account.
  3. Keep collection scope and frequency reasonable, following platform rules, applicable laws, and privacy requirements.

3. Validate the Exported Data

After exporting to CSV or Excel, check field completeness, duplicate records, and whether timestamps, text, and engagement fields look as expected. The number of collected rows alone is not a measure of data quality.

Useful Fields to Track

  • Video URL or ID: trace each comment to its source.
  • Comment text: support topic analysis and manual review.
  • Comment timestamp: observe discussion changes.
  • Public engagement metrics: provide context, with metric definitions noted.
  • Collection timestamp: identify the data snapshot for comparison.

4. Organize the Data in Excel

  1. Remove exact duplicates while keeping an untouched copy of the raw export.
  2. Filter comments by video, date, or keyword.
  3. Flag irrelevant, blank, or abnormal records and document the cleaning rules.
  4. Review a sample against the original videos to verify context.
Comments may contain personal information. Apply data minimization and avoid publicly sharing information that could identify individuals.

5. Make the Workflow Reusable

Keep the keywords, video list, collection dates, exported files, and cleaning rules for every project. This makes it possible to compare results when the dataset is updated.
