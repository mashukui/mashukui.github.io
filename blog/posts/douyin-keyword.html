抖音关键词采集怎么做？关键词分组与结果筛选

2026-11-22 · 马哥数据采集

介绍如何从业务问题拆解关键词、管理搜索批次、筛选视频结果并记录数据来源。

一、把业务问题转成关键词

从品牌词、产品词、场景词、问题词、竞品词和活动词六类开始。每组词对应一个研究意图，例如了解产品体验时，场景词和问题词通常比宽泛行业词更有针对性。

二、建立关键词表

建议字段包括关键词、词组类别、目标意图、添加日期、优先级、搜索批次和备注。为关键词设置统一命名规则，方便后续比较不同批次的结果。

三、先小范围试搜

每组先选少量代表词，观察返回内容是否相关、是否过于宽泛，以及是否存在同义词。根据结果调整词组，不要只追求搜索词数量。

四、筛选结果并记录原因

可以按内容相关性、发布时间、账号或互动区间筛选，但每个条件都应服务于研究问题。保留原始搜索词和筛选理由，避免结果表失去来源信息。

五、避免重复与偏差

多个关键词可能命中同一视频，建议使用稳定的视频链接或标识去重，同时保留该视频命中的全部关键词。不同关键词的结果量不能直接代表真实话题热度，因为排序和推荐机制会影响可见结果。

搜索结果是平台在特定时间和条件下呈现的样本，不等同于全量内容。报告中应说明采集日期、关键词和筛选范围。

Douyin Keyword Research: Grouping and Filtering Search Results

2026-11-22 · Mage Data Tools

Learn to turn business questions into keyword groups, manage search batches, filter video results, and record data sources.

1. Turn Business Questions into Keywords

Start with six groups: brand terms, product terms, use-case terms, problem terms, competitor terms, and campaign terms. Each group should serve a research intent. For product-experience research, use-case and problem terms are often more targeted than broad industry terms.

2. Maintain a Keyword Sheet

Suggested fields include keyword, group, search intent, date added, priority, search batch, and notes. Use consistent naming so results from different batches can be compared.

3. Test a Small Search Set First

Choose a few representative terms from each group and review relevance, breadth, and synonyms in the results. Refine the groups based on what you find rather than maximizing the number of search terms.

4. Filter Results and Record the Reason

Filter by content relevance, publication date, creator, or engagement range when those criteria serve the research question. Keep the original search term and selection rationale so the result table retains its provenance.

5. Avoid Duplicates and Sampling Bias

Multiple keywords may return the same video. Deduplicate by a stable video URL or identifier while retaining all keywords that matched it. Result counts for different keywords do not directly represent topic popularity because ranking and recommendation systems affect what is visible.

Search results are samples shown by the platform under specific conditions and at a specific time, not a complete census. Document collection dates, keywords, and filtering scope in your report.
