微博关键词数据怎么整理？从搜索结果到舆情表格

2026-11-24 · 马哥数据采集

从关键词分组、时间范围设定，到博文与评论采集、字段核对和表格分析，整理一套可复用的微博舆情数据流程。

一、先明确监测目标

先写清楚要回答的问题：品牌声量、事件走势，还是竞品对比。目标决定关键词、时间范围和需要采集的字段，避免一上来就大规模抓取。

二、设计关键词与时间范围

  1. 核心词加同义词、缩写和常见错写，减少漏采。
  2. 准备排除词，过滤明显无关的内容。
  3. 按事件阶段分时间段检索，并记录每次使用的检索式。

三、采集哪些字段

博文层面建议保留：博文链接、作者、发布时间、正文、话题标签、转发数、评论数、点赞数和 IP 属地；评论层面保留评论者、评论内容、评论时间、点赞数和评论级别。转发、评论、点赞的口径要提前确认，避免分析时混用。

四、导出后的检查

  1. 删除重复博文和重复评论，保留原始备份。
  2. 检查时间范围、空值和乱码。
  3. 抽样打开原博文，核对正文、互动数据和评论是否对应。
  4. 把明显营销号、无关话题单独标记，不要直接删除。
舆情数据里可能包含个人信息和未经证实的说法。引用时保留原文链接，避免断章取义，也不要公开传播可识别个人身份的信息。

五、分析口径与呈现

先看声量趋势和话题分布，再做主题分类和情绪标注；互动量受账号体量和时间影响，只能作为参考。报告中保留代表性样本和原文链接，方便复核。

六、合规与留存

只处理公开数据，遵守平台规则和适用法律；保留检索式、时间范围、导出文件和清洗规则，方便复现和更新。

How to Organize Weibo Keyword Data into a Research Table

2026-11-24 · Mage Data Tools

A practical workflow for keyword groups, time windows, post and comment collection, field checks, and analysis tables.

1. Define the Monitoring Goal

Start with the question you need to answer: brand visibility, the arc of an event, or a competitor comparison. The goal determines keywords, time range, and fields, and prevents unnecessary large-scale collection.

2. Design Keywords and Time Windows

  1. Combine core terms with synonyms, abbreviations, and common misspellings to reduce gaps.
  2. Prepare exclusion terms to filter obviously irrelevant posts.
  3. Search in stages across the event timeline, and record each query you use.

3. Choose the Fields

For posts, keep the URL, author, publish time, text, hashtags, reposts, comments, likes, and IP location. For comments, keep the commenter, comment text, comment time, likes, and comment level. Confirm how reposts, comments, and likes are counted before analysis so the metrics are not mixed.

4. Check the Export

  1. Remove duplicate posts and comments, and keep a raw backup.
  2. Check the time range, empty values, and encoding issues.
  3. Open a sample of original posts to verify text, engagement, and comments.
  4. Flag obvious marketing accounts and off-topic posts instead of deleting them.
Public-opinion data may include personal information and unverified claims. Keep the original links when citing, avoid quoting out of context, and do not publicly share identifiable personal information.

5. Analysis and Presentation

Start with volume trends and topic distribution, then add theme categories and sentiment labels. Engagement is affected by account size and timing, so treat it as a reference only. Keep representative samples and original links in the report for review.

6. Compliance and Record-Keeping

Only process public data and follow platform rules and applicable laws. Keep the queries, time ranges, exported files, and cleaning rules so the work can be reproduced and updated.
